## What it is

When you accept a quote, we offer you an **optional** checkbox. If you tick it, you allow us to **reuse the model or drawing made for you** in other projects and in our **catalogue**.

## What you authorise

By ticking the box you authorise us, on a non-exclusive basis, to:

- reuse the model or drawing made for your order, including adapting it, in other projects of the business;
- include it in the catalogue of repeatable products and to produce and sell it to other customers;
- keep it in our internal model archive.

The authorisation covers the **model or drawing that we made**. It does not cover your personal data, nor files, trademarks or designs you own and gave us, nor third-party elements, unless you expressly tell us otherwise. ## It is optional

- You may **not** tick the box: your order goes ahead anyway, on the same terms.
- It does not affect the price, **unless otherwise stated** in the quote (for example a discount we may offer if you give consent).
- Without your consent we do not reuse the model or add it to the catalogue: the archive of reusable models contains only models with consent.

## How to withdraw it

You can withdraw consent **at any time** by writing to [EMAIL_PRIVACY]. Withdrawal applies **going forward**: from then on we will not use the model in new projects and will remove it from the catalogue. It does not affect uses already made or products already made or sold to others before withdrawal. [TO BE VALIDATED: effects of withdrawal on rights already granted.]

## What we record

To be able to prove your consent, we store: the **text and version** of the consent shown, the **date and time** of acceptance, and the link to your order. This data is processed under the [privacy policy](privacy.en.md).

This consent is separate from the [terms and conditions](termini.en.md) and is not a condition for ordering.
