## 2. What the site does at this stage

At this stage the site **does not sell anything**. You can sign up to an **interest list**: you tell us what you need and we contact you when we are operational. Signing up is not an order and does not bind you.

We do not ask for special categories of data (for example health data). Please do not enter them in free-text fields.

## 4. Why we use it and on what legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Managing your interest-list sign-up and answering your requests | Art. 6(1)(b): steps taken at your request before entering into a contract |

We do not make automated decisions or carry out profiling.

## 5. Customer area access (email link)

When you sign up, we automatically create an account linked to your email. There is no password: each time you want to log in, we email you a personal, time-limited **login link** (magic link). Anyone with access to your inbox can use the link, so keep your email secure.

## 6. Uploaded files

Files you upload in the customer area are stored in the cloud (Supabase Storage, in a private bucket). They are then **copied to an internal server of the business**, where they are kept with an **encrypted backup**. Once the copy is confirmed, the files are **deleted from the cloud within 30 days**.

Only upload files that you are entitled to share with us.

Some providers are companies with offices or infrastructure also outside the EU. When data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area, we rely on the tools provided by the GDPR, in particular the European Commission's **standard contractual clauses** or the provider's certification under the EU-US **Data Privacy Framework**, where applicable. You can ask about the safeguards in place by writing to [EMAIL_PRIVACY].

## 9. How long we keep data

- **Sign-ups with no follow-up:** deleted after **24 months**.
- **Order data:** kept for **10 years from the activation of the company**, for tax and accounting obligations.
- **Uploaded files:** deleted from the cloud within 30 days of the confirmed copy to the internal server; on the internal server they follow the retention of the order they relate to.
- **Technical security data:** only as long as strictly necessary, according to provider settings.
- **Account:** you can delete it at any time (see below).

If you ask for deletion but some data must be kept by law, we keep it only for that purpose and for the required period.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Arts. 15-21 GDPR). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

- **Delete your account:** use the "Delete my account" button in the customer area.
- **Other requests:** write to [EMAIL_PRIVACY]. We reply within one month.
- **Complaints:** you can contact the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or the authority of your country of residence.

## 11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures: encrypted connections, role-based access, private storage, encrypted backups. No system is 100% secure: in case of a data breach we will make the notifications required by law.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes we will notify you.
