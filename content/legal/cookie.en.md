## Why no banner is needed

Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003), which implements the ePrivacy Directive, requires prior consent for cookies that are not strictly necessary. **Technical cookies**, needed to provide the service you request (such as staying logged in or remembering your language), do not require consent: this notice is enough.

If we later use other cookies (for example tracking analytics or advertising), we will update this page and ask for your consent before installing them.
